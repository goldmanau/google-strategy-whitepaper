# 📊 The Success of Google — From Search Infrastructure to AI-Driven Dominance (1998–2030)

## 📌 Overview
This whitepaper provides a strategic and financial analysis of Google’s growth from 1998 to 2026 and its projected trajectory to 2030.

It examines:
- How Google transformed from a search engine into a global revenue leader
- Why its infrastructure-first strategy outperformed competitors
- Financial comparison with Microsoft and Yahoo
- Future outlook in AI and cloud computing

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

- Google’s early focus on **search and infrastructure (not revenue)** enabled long-term dominance
- Advertising based on **user intent** created a highly scalable business model
- Microsoft remained strong in enterprise, but **Google surpassed it in global revenue scale**
- AI and cloud will define the next phase of competition
